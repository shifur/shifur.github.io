Shifur Rahman Shakil

PhD Student & Graduate Research Assistant
Florida State University

I am a PhD student in the Department of Scientific Computing at Florida State University, advised by Dr. Nick Dexter and Dr. Hristo Chipilski. My research focuses on Bayesian inverse problems, uncertainty quantification and scientific machine learning.

I develop surrogate models using deep neural networks and sparse polynomial approximations to enable efficient Bayesian inference and optimal observation design. I also investigate satellite-based wildfire detection and multi-sensor data fusion using Landsat, VIIRS and MODIS observations.

Before joining FSU, I earned an MSc in Data Analytics at Queen Mary University of London as a Chevening Scholar. Previously, I served as a Senior Manager at BRAC, the world’s largest non-governmental organization (NGO), leading the technology, monitoring and evaluation, and research teams of its Skills Development Programme (approximately US$20 million annual budget). I led over 40 research projects and the development and deployment of digital products for approximately 1,500 field staff, and developed four inclusive technology products for Uganda, Sierra Leone, Tanzania and Liberia. Prior to my senior management role, I worked as a Research Associate at BRAC University.
