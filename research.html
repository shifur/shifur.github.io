AI-based surrogate modeling

My research develops computational surrogates for expensive cloud-column simulations to support Bayesian calibration of cloud microphysics parameters. I study how approximation quality translates into posterior accuracy and inference efficiency.

  • Deep neural networks and sparse polynomial approximation using compressed sensing.
  • Learning curves, multi-output response approximation and sensitivity analysis.
  • Evaluation of posterior fidelity and computational efficiency against full-model inference.

Sampling complexity and optimal observation design

I study the relationship between simulation budgets, observation selection and the geometry of nonlinear inverse problems. The goal is to understand how accurate a surrogate must be for reliable scientific inference.

  • Joint simulation and observation complexity.
  • Christoffel-guided sampling and adaptive observation design.
  • Identifiability, response-space geometry and surrogate-assisted Bayesian inference.

Satellite wildfire detection and multi-sensor fusion

My Earth observation research examines near-coincident Landsat, VIIRS and MODIS observations to develop reliable methods for active-fire detection, validation and cross-sensor fusion.

  • Match observation timing and native spatial footprints.
  • Build independent Landsat-based reference evidence without treating an operational detection product as ground truth.
  • Evaluate sensor agreement and characterize uncertainty before multi-sensor fusion.
